### Pattern (4/4)

```
1  +  2  +  3  +  4  +
P  i  m  i  P  i  m  i
      a           a
```


**Legend:**
- **P** = Thumb (bass strings: E, A, D)
- **i** = Index (G string)
- **m** = Middle (B string)
- **a** = Ring (high E string)
